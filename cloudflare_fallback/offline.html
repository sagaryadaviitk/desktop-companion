What does Cloudflare error 1033 mean?

Cloudflare can’t find a healthy tunnel connector on your Mac. Error 502 can mean the tunnel is connected but the local service isn’t responding. A restricted network, expired captive-portal session, restart, system sleep, or lost power can interrupt access.

Saved Wi-Fi networks need Auto-Join enabled. Actual system sleep, shutdown, depleted battery, and login/FileVault screens can prevent Companion from running until macOS is awake and logged in.
